# Privacy policy

Spoiler Shield does not collect, store remotely, sell or share any data.

**What it reads.** To find spoilers, the extension reads the text and image descriptions (alt text, titles, labels) of the pages you visit. That happens inside your browser, on your device. Page content is never saved and never sent anywhere.

**What it stores.** Your settings: the keyword list, on/off state, blur or hide mode, the page warning toggle and the sites you paused it on. They're kept in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome syncs them between your own browsers through your Google account, the same way it syncs bookmarks. The extension itself has no server and no access to that sync data beyond your own settings.

**Network.** The extension makes no network requests. No analytics, no tracking, no remote code.

**Permissions.**

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Saves your settings. |
| Content script on all sites | Spoilers can show up on any site, so it needs to read pages to blur them. |

**Questions.** Open an issue at https://github.com/jasonalsaqqa/spoiler-shield/issues.
